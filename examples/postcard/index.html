The postcard you wrote flew away as dust, and this receipt came back from it. Every speck carried a piece of the live page.

A real HTML form, drawn into a WebGPU texture by Chrome's experimental HTML-in-Canvas API whenever it repaints, then bent by TypeGPU shaders. It stays a form. Type, pick a stamp, press Send.

interstage's part is small. The six shaders are .wgsl files, and their structs and bind group layouts reach TypeScript as types.

This demo needs HTML-in-Canvas

In Chrome or another Chromium browser, turn on chrome://flags/#canvas-draw-element, then relaunch. Here is the card as plain HTML in the meantime.
